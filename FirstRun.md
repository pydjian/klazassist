# Welcome to KlazAssist 👋

**A quick start guide for first-time users**

---

## What is KlazAssist?

KlazAssist is a **classroom management app for teachers**. It helps you keep track of:

- 👥 Your learners and their information
- ✅ Daily attendance
- 📊 Grades and assessments
- 🪑 Seating arrangements and group activities
- 📚 Lesson plans and calendars

Everything lives **on your own device** — no servers, no accounts to create elsewhere, no one else can see your data. It works even without internet.

> **Think of it as your digital teaching notebook** — but one that never runs out of pages.

---

## Before You Begin

You'll need:

- ✅ A modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari)
- ✅ About 10 minutes to set up
- ✅ Your class list handy (optional, but helpful)

> **Tip:** KlazAssist works best when you **install it to your home screen** (like an app). We'll show you how in Step 3.

## Step 1 — Create Your Local Account

The first time you open KlazAssist, you'll see a welcome wizard.

### What to do

1. **Read the intro screen** — it explains how your data stays private. Click **Continue**.

2. **Set up your account:**
   - **Your Name** — how you want to be referred to (e.g. "Maria Santos")
   - **Username** — something easy to remember (e.g. `msantos`)
   - **Password** — at least 8 characters. Mix letters, numbers, and a symbol for strength.
   - Confirm your password.

3. **Choose your security preferences:**
   - **Auto-lock** — we recommend **15 minutes**. This locks the app if you walk away from your computer.
   - **Remember this device** — leave checked if this is your personal computer.

4. **Save your recovery key** — this is **very important**.
   - KlazAssist will show you a key like `ABCD-1234-EFGH-5678`.
   - Click **Download .txt** or **Copy** and store it somewhere safe.
   - **This is the only way to reset your password** if you forget it. There's no "forgot password" email — because there's no server to send one from.

5. **Check the box** confirming you've saved your recovery key. Then click **Finish setup**.

> ⚠️ **Don't skip the recovery key.** Write it down, print it, or save it in your password manager. If you lose your password *and* your recovery key, your account cannot be recovered.

## Step 2 — Set Up Your School and Class

After login, you'll see a **Setup Wizard**. It walks you through:

### Teacher Profile
- Your name, position, contact details
- You can skip this and fill it in later — but adding it now means it appears on your printed reports.

### School Profile
- School name, ID, division, region
- **This appears on every report you print** — SF1, SF2, SF9, class lists.

### Your First Class

- **Section** (e.g. Saint Claire)
- **Subject** (e.g. Mathematics)
- **School Year** (e.g. 2026-2027)

### Final choice: Add learners now or explore first?
- **Add Learners Now** — jumps straight into adding your class list
- **Explore with Sample Data** — loads 25 fake learners so you can try everything without risk
- **Skip for now** — takes you to the dashboard

> 💡 **New to the app?** Choose **Explore with Sample Data**. You can delete it any time from Settings → Data.

## Step 3 — Install KlazAssist Like an App

KlazAssist works fine in a browser tab, but installing it gives you three benefits:

1. **It works offline** even without opening the browser first
2. **Your data is protected** — the browser won't delete it under disk pressure
3. **It feels like a real app** — one tap from your home screen

### On Windows / Mac (Chrome or Edge)
1. Look for the **install icon** in the address bar (looks like a monitor with a down arrow)
2. Click it → **Install**

### On iPhone / iPad (Safari)
1. Tap the **Share** button (square with an up arrow)
2. Scroll down → **Add to Home Screen**
3. Tap **Add**

### On Android (Chrome)
1. Tap the **three-dot menu** in the top-right
2. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**

> ⚠️ **Important for iPhone users:** Safari deletes app data after 7 days of not using it. **Install to Home Screen** to prevent this — otherwise you risk losing your data.

## Step 4 — Your First Week

Here's a simple path to get comfortable with KlazAssist in your first week.

### Day 1: Get your class in
- [ ] Go to **Learners** → click **+ Add Learner**
- [ ] Add each learner (you can also **Import from SF1** if you have the file)
- [ ] At minimum, add **first name, last name, and sex**

### Day 2: Record attendance
- [ ] Go to **Daily Attendance**
- [ ] Pick today's date (it defaults to today)
- [ ] Mark each learner — **Present**, **Absent**, **Late**, or **Excused**
- [ ] Click the **Save** button (bottom-right of the screen, or at the bottom of the table)

> 💡 **Speed tip:** Tap **Mark All Present** first, then change only the absent or late learners.

### Day 3: Create an assessment
- [ ] Go to **Assessment Builder** → **+ New Assessment**
- [ ] Title it (e.g. "Quiz 1 — Fractions")
- [ ] Choose the category (Written Work, Performance Task, or Exam)
- [ ] Set the maximum score

### Day 4: Enter some grades
- [ ] Go to **Gradebook**
- [ ] Find your new assessment column
- [ ] Click any cell and type the score
- [ ] Press Enter — it saves automatically

### Day 5: Try the fun tools
- [ ] **Wheel of Names** — spin to pick a random student
- [ ] **Timer** — countdown for activities
- [ ] **Randomizer** — dice, coin flips, random numbers

### Day 6: Back up your data
- [ ] Go to **Export Center** → **Backup All Data**
- [ ] Save the `.json` file somewhere safe — like Google Drive or a USB stick

### Day 7: Print something
- [ ] Go to **Class List** → **Print Options**
- [ ] Try the **Standard Class List** layout
- [ ] Print or save as PDF

## The 5 Things You'll Do Most

### 1. Mark Attendance

**Daily Attendance → click status buttons → Save**

The screen shows all your learners. Click **Present**, **Absent**, **Late**, or **Excused** for each. There's a note field if you need to add context.

### 2. Add a Learner

**Learners → + Add Learner → fill in details → Save**

Only the first and last names are required. Everything else (LRN, birth date, address, parent contact) is optional but helps with SF1/SF2 exports later.

### 3. Enter Grades

**Gradebook → click a cell → type score → press Enter**

The table shows one column per assessment. Cells save automatically when you click elsewhere or press Enter.

### 4. Take Notes

**Notes → + New Note**

Write anything — lesson ideas, reminders, meeting notes. Pin the important ones to keep them at the top.

### 5. Pick a Random Student

**Teaching Tools → Random Student Picker → Pick Student**

If you check **"Avoid recently selected learners"**, everyone gets picked before anyone repeats.

## Protecting Your Data

Your data lives **only on this device**. That's private and fast, but it also means **you** are responsible for backup.

### The three things to remember

**1. Back up regularly**
- Go to **Export Center → Backup All Data** at least once a week
- Store the `.json` file outside KlazAssist — on Google Drive, OneDrive, a USB stick, or email it to yourself

**2. Use the app lock**
- **Settings → Security → Auto-lock after inactivity**
- Set this to 15 minutes so the app locks if you walk away

**3. Don't clear your browser data**
- Clearing "site data" or "cookies and cache" in your browser settings will **delete KlazAssist's data**
- If you must clear browser data, back up first

> 💡 **Rule of thumb:** If you wouldn't want to lose it, back it up.

## Common Questions

### Can I use KlazAssist on my phone and laptop at the same time?

Yes — but they'll have **separate data**. Each device keeps its own copy. To move data between them:

1. On Device A: **Export Center → Backup All Data**
2. Transfer the file to Device B (email, USB, cloud)
3. On Device B: **Export Center → Restore from JSON**

### What happens if I forget my password?

Use your **recovery key** (the one from Step 1).

On the login screen, click **Account recovery → Enter recovery key → Set new password**.

If you've lost both password and recovery key, access cannot be recovered — this is intentional, because KlazAssist has no server to verify your identity against.

### Will my data be deleted if I don't open the app for a while?

- **On Android / Chrome / Firefox:** No, your data is safe.
- **On iPhone / Safari:** **Yes, after 7 days.** Safari treats web app data as temporary unless you've installed the app to your Home Screen.

**Fix:** Install KlazAssist to your Home Screen (Step 3).

### Can I share my license with another teacher?

A **Pro license is per-teacher**. If you share it, printed reports will show the license holder's name — not the other teacher's. Each teacher should have their own license.

### Does KlazAssist send my data anywhere?

No. KlazAssist stores everything locally.

The only exception: **AI features** (Lesson Planner, TOS Generator, PowerPoint Generator) send the text you paste to Google's Gemini API — and only when you click Generate. **Learner names, grades, and personal information are never included.**

## Appendix: What "Offline-First" Really Means

KlazAssist is built around three promises:

1. **Your data stays on your device.** No cloud, no sign-up, no third party.
2. **No internet is required.** Everything works on a plane, in a basement, or during a power outage.
3. **You are in control.** You decide what to back up, where to store it, and who gets to see your screen.

The only network calls the app ever makes are:

- **AI features** (opt-in — you paste the text)
- **Email sharing** (opt-in — you click Send)
- **Font and library loading** (only during initial setup)

You can block all three and KlazAssist will still work perfectly for daily teaching.
