## Appendix: Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Ctrl + K` (or `Cmd + K`) | Open global search |
| `Space` (on Wheel of Names page) | Spin the wheel |
| `Esc` | Close any modal or dialog |
| `Enter` (in a gradebook cell) | Save the score and move to the next cell |

## Appendix: What "Offline-First" Really Means

KlazAssist is built around three promises:

1. **Your data stays on your device.** No cloud, no sign-up, no third party.
2. **No internet is required.** Everything works on a plane, in a basement, or during a power outage.
3. **You are in control.** You decide what to back up, where to store it, and who gets to see your screen.

The only network calls the app ever makes are:

- **AI features** (opt-in — you paste the text)
- **Email sharing** (opt-in — you click Send)
- **Font and library loading** (only during initial setup)

You can block all three and KlazAssist will still work perfectly for daily teaching.
